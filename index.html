Explore services

Four ways we help young people move forward

Supported living

A shared home with staff on site day and night.

  • Named keyworker
  • Individual support plan
  • Regular reviews with the placing authority

Education support

On-site learning plus links to schools and colleges.

  • Skills and Development Centre
  • ASDAN-accredited courses
  • Help with uniform, equipment and transport

Independent living skills

Practical skills for running a home.

  • Cooking and healthy eating
  • Budgeting and money management
  • Travel training

Community engagement

Belonging, friendships and local opportunities.

  • Volunteering and work experience
  • Sport and leisure
  • Places of worship and youth groups
