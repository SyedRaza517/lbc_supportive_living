Supporting families every step of the way

When it is right for the person, we want families involved: visiting, joining reviews and celebrating progress together.

Your first visitWhat to expectStaying involvedQuestionsContact the team

Come and see the home

  • A member of staff shows you round the house and the person's room.
  • You meet the keyworker and can ask anything you want.
  • We explain house rules, visiting and how we keep in touch.
  • Visits are arranged with the person and their social worker.
Arrange a visit

The journey, step by step

  1. ReferralThe social worker refers the person to us.
  2. AssessmentWe get to know the person and their needs.
  3. Move inA planned welcome and a named keyworker.
  4. Support planningGoals agreed with the person.
  5. ReviewsRegular reviews of progress, with family invited where appropriate.

Ways to stay involved

Review meetings

Join reviews to hear about progress and share your views.

Communication

Regular updates from the keyworker, agreed with the person.

Visits

Visits arranged at times that suit the person and the household.

Activities

Birthdays, achievements and events, celebrated together.

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Call us on 020 8259 6298.

Can I visit whenever I like?

Visits are arranged in advance with the person, their social worker and the team, so they suit everyone living in the home.

Will I be told how they are getting on?

Yes, where the person agrees and it is in line with their care plan. You may also be invited to reviews.

Who is the main contact?

The person's named keyworker, supported by the project manager.

Is someone there at night?

Yes. Staff are on site 24 hours a day, every day.

What if I am worried about something?

Call us at any time on 020 8259 6298. You can also make a complaint using our complaints procedure. If a child is in immediate danger, call 999.
