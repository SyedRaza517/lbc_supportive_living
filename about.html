About LBC Supported Living

LBC Supported Living Services provides supported accommodation, education and independent living skills for adults at 79 Watford Way, Hendon.

Our storyOur valuesLeadership teamSafeguardingQuality and compliancePolicies

From a single idea to a home people are proud of

We believe care leavers and adults who need support deserve the same patient, practical preparation for independent life that most families give their own children.

  1. EstablishmentLBC Supported Living Services is set up to give people a stable home with education built in.
  2. GrowthA team of support workers and keyworkers forms around a person-centred, coaching-based approach using the GROW model.
  3. Property development79 Watford Way is refurbished: eight en-suite bedrooms, a lounge, kitchen and dining room, activity room and gated garden.
  4. Current services24-hour on-site support, an on-site Skills and Development Centre, ASDAN-accredited life skills and community activities.
  5. Future visionMore step-down, semi-independent places, so people can move on gradually with the same team behind them.

Our mission

To help every person in our care build the confidence, skills and qualifications they need to live independently and safely.

Our vision

A future where every care-experienced person leaves supported accommodation with a plan, a support network and real belief in themselves.

Six values, lived every day

Dignity

Every person is treated with respect for who they are and where they have come from.

Respect

We listen, we explain our decisions and we keep our promises.

Independence

We do things with people, so they can do them on their own.

Choice

People make decisions about their daily lives and their plans.

Safety

A secure home, safe recruitment and staff on site around the clock.

Achievement

Every step forward is recognised, from a first meal cooked to a qualification gained.

Keeping people safe is the first thing we do, every shift

  • Trained, experienced staff, including positive handling, equality and diversity, and health and safety training.
  • A secure building with fire and intruder alarms, CCTV, door chimes and a fenced, gated garden and car park.
  • Missing person procedures, so the right people are told quickly if someone is missing and at risk.
  • Openness: we notify CQC of serious incidents and safeguarding concerns, as required.
  • Listening to people through keywork sessions and a clear complaints process.

Worried about a person right now? Call 020 8259 6298 at any time. If a child is in immediate danger, call 999.

Built around CQC's five key questions

The Care Quality Commission (CQC) inspects care services against five key questions: are they safe, effective, caring, responsive and well-led?

Well-led

Clear governance, a registered manager and regular quality review.

Safe

Safeguarding, a secure home, safe recruitment and notification of serious incidents.

Effective

Support plans built on assessed needs, with goals reviewed and progress tracked.

Caring

Dignity, respect and a named keyworker who gets to know each person well.

Responsive

Support that changes as people's needs and goals change, and a clear complaints process.
