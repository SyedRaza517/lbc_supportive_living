Placement criteria

Who we can support

Every referral is assessed individually. If we are not the right match, we will say so quickly.

Age range

Young people in care or leaving care. Confirm the age range

Needs we support

Mild to moderate learning disabilities, low-level emotional and mental health needs, and young people who need help to build independent living skills.

Placement types

Planned placements, step-down from residential care and, subject to matching, emergency placements.

When we may not be the right match

Where a young person's assessed needs or risks are beyond what a shared, supported setting can safely manage. We will talk this through with you openly.

Referral process

From first call to move-in

  1. ReferralCall, email or use the online form.
  2. AssessmentWe review documents and speak with you.
  3. MatchingWe consider the young person and the current household.
  4. OfferWritten confirmation and proposed start date.
  5. Move inA planned welcome with a named keyworker.
Start a referral

Matching procedure

We look at the young person's needs, strengths, risks and wishes alongside the needs of the young people already living at the home, so the household works for everyone.

Safeguarding approach

Trained staff on site 24 hours, missing-from-placement procedures, CCTV and alarms, and notification of serious incidents to Ofsted and the placing authority.

Outcomes framework

How we measure progress

Goals are agreed with the young person and shared with you at every review.

Education and training
Attendance, engagement and qualifications, including ASDAN and Functional Skills.
Independent living
Cooking, budgeting, shopping, cleaning and travel, recorded as skills are achieved.
Health and wellbeing
Registration with a GP and dentist, attending appointments, emotional wellbeing.
Relationships and community
Positive relationships, family contact where appropriate, activities and volunteering.
Moving on
A planned next step, prepared with the young person and their personal adviser.
