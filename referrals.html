How it works

Five steps from referral to moving in

We keep you updated at every stage and tell you quickly if we are not the right match.

  1. Submit referral

    Send the referral form and supporting documents.

  2. Assessment

    We read the referral, talk to you and, where possible, meet the young person.

  3. Placement matching

    We check the young person's needs against the current household and our support.

  4. Placement offer

    We confirm in writing, with a proposed move-in date and support plan outline.

  5. Move in

    A planned welcome, with the named keyworker introduced beforehand wherever possible.

Referral criteria

Who we can accept

Every referral is assessed individually. If we are not the right match, we tell you quickly.

Full placement criteria for social workers
  • Referred by a local authority social work or leaving care team.
  • Young people in care or leaving care. Confirm the age range
  • Needs we support: independent living skills, education, low-level emotional and mental health needs, and mild to moderate learning disabilities.
  • Placement types: planned, step-down from residential care and, subject to matching, emergency.
