    What's it like living here?

    • Your own room with an en-suite bathroom.
    • Your keyworker: one person who gets to know you and helps you with your plan.
    • Someone is always here, day and night.
    • House meetings where you get a say in how the home runs.
    • Your own goals, set by you, not for you.

    Every step forward counts

    We celebrate achievements big and small.

    Certificates

    ASDAN awards, first aid, food hygiene.

    Qualifications

    GCSEs, Functional Skills, college courses.

    Volunteering

    Giving time to local projects.

    Employment success

    First jobs, apprenticeships and work experience.

    If something is wrong, you can tell someone

    You can talk to any member of staff, your keyworker or your social worker. You can also make a complaint. These free helplines are there too.

    Samaritans (free, 24/7)116 123If you need someone to talk to
    NHS 111111Urgent health advice. Choose the mental health option if you need it
    Emergency999If you or someone else is in danger
