Education

Learning that fits you, at our on-site Skills and Development Centre or at a local college or training provider.

Talk to someone All opportunities

What you can do

  • GCSE support and Functional Skills English and Maths
  • ASDAN-accredited courses
  • Help to find and apply for college courses
  • Homework space and support in the activity room

Getting started, step by step

  1. Step 1Choose what you want to learn with your keyworker
  2. Step 2Start with short sessions that build confidence
  3. Step 3Get help with uniform, equipment and travel
  4. Step 4Celebrate certificates as you earn them

Ask your keyworker to add education to your support plan. It is your plan, so you choose your goals.
